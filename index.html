Start with the team workbook

  1. Open the Google Sheet

    2026 Internal Analysis Dashboard Data (opens in a new tab)

    Use the Google account that has access to the team workbook. If you cannot open it, ask Isaac for access.

  2. Download the full workbook

    In Google Sheets, select File, then Download, then Microsoft Excel (.xlsx). Keep all the tabs together.

  3. Upload and choose a survey

    Drop the downloaded file here or select Choose workbook. Choose a survey, then scroll through its questions. Add a breakdown to compare responses by another question, site characteristic, or grantee characteristic.

Download a fresh copy when you return. The workbook’s Refresh Status tab lists the source and publication dates for each dataset.
